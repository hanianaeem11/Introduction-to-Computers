 ## History of Computers
 **19th Century (Mechanical Foundations):**
 <p> *Charles Babbage designed the Analytical Engine, introducing the core concepts of input, output, memory, and programmable computing logic.*
**1940s (Vacuum Tubes & ENIAC):** </br>
*The invention of vacuum tubes made fully electronic computing possible, leading to room-sized machines like the ENIAC used for complex military calculations.*
**1950s (Transistor Revolution):**</br>
*Transistors replaced bulky, heat-generating vacuum tubes, allowing computers to become smaller, faster, cheaper, and significantly more reliable.*
**1970s (Microprocessors & PCs):**</br>
*The placement of thousands of transistors onto a single silicon chip (microprocessor) kicked off the Personal Computer (PC) revolution, bringing machines into homes and offices.*</br>
**2000s–Present (Mobile & Cloud Era):**</br>
*Miniaturization and global internet networking transformed computers into modern smartphones, cloud data centers, and advanced AI systems.*</br>
## Types of Computer</br>
**Microcomputers (Personal Computers):**</br>
*Designed for individual use, including desktop PCs, laptops, tablets, and smartphones used for everyday tasks, work, and communication.*</br>
**Minicomputers (Midrange Computers):**</br>
*Medium-sized servers capable of supporting multiple users simultaneously, often used by small to mid-sized businesses for managing local databases and applications.*</br>
**Supercomputers:**</br>
*The fastest and most powerful computers on Earth, built to perform trillions of complex calculations per second for scientific research, weather forecasting, and AI training.* </p>
